Services

Our approach combines structured
methodology with practical execution

We help IT companies transform financial data into reporting that meets international standards.

01

Local GAAP → IFRS Transformation

Full transformation of financial reporting from Ukrainian accounting standards to IFRS. Adjusting entries, reclassifications, explanatory notes.

02

Full IFRS Reporting Package

Preparation of a complete set of financial statements: statement of financial position, statement of profit or loss and OCI, cash flow statement, statement of changes in equity, and notes.

03

Audit & Due Diligence Support

Preparation of documentation and working files for audit reviews. Communication with auditors, responding to queries, preparing additional disclosures.

04

Investor-Ready Structuring

Preparation of financial information in a format understood by international investors, funds, and strategic partners. Management reporting, KPI dashboards.

Our Approach

Structured Methodology
Instead of Manual Work

IFRS reporting at many companies still takes months. The main challenge lies not so much in the IFRS requirements themselves, but in the lack of a structured approach to transformation.

  • Structured reconciliation of financial data between standards
  • Consistent and well-grounded logic for transformation adjustments
  • Centralized transformation using automated tools
  • Additional layers of calculation accuracy controls

Process

How We Work

Four clear stages from analyzing your data to IFRS-ready financial statements.

01

Analysis

We study your accounting system, identify differences between local GAAP and IFRS, and develop a transformation plan.

Output: Transformation Plan
02

Transformation

We perform adjusting entries, prepare reclassifications, and build transformation worksheets.

Output: Working Files
03

Reporting

We prepare a complete set of IFRS financial statements, including notes and disclosures.

Output: 5 Financial Statements
04

Audit

We prepare working files for auditors, respond to queries, and support the review process.

Output: Clean Opinion
