FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does an IFRS financial reporting transformation take?

The duration depends on the volume of accounting data, the complexity of business transactions, the number of reporting periods and the readiness of the source information. For some projects the estimated timeline may be 2–6 weeks; however, exact timelines are set after a preliminary assessment.

Which documents are needed to start a transformation?

To start, we usually need the trial balance, the financial statements for the relevant periods, the accounting policy and a breakdown of material accounting items. Any further documents depend on the specifics of the company's activities and the results of the preliminary diagnostics.

What does the cost of a transformation depend on?

The cost depends on the scale of the company's operations, the complexity of its business transactions, the number of reporting periods, the quality of the source data and the volume of adjustments required. After a preliminary review of the company's needs, we define the scope of work and prepare an individual commercial proposal.

How does a financial reporting transformation differ from an audit?

A transformation brings financial information into line with IFRS requirements, including identifying the necessary adjustments and preparing the financial statements. An audit is an independent examination of the financial statements in order to express an auditor's opinion on them.

Do you work with companies outside the IT sector?

Yes, FTH Advisory provides services to companies from various sectors of the economy.
