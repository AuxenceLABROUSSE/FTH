IFRS reporting transformation in Kyiv

Financial Transformation Hub (FTH) is a Kyiv-based team that transforms company financial reporting from Ukrainian accounting standards (UAS) to IFRS. In Kyiv, we can meet you in person.

Meet in person in Kyiv

You can discuss your project face to face. The first consultation is free: it helps us understand your needs and estimate the scope of work. Write to contact@fthadvisory.com or use the contact form, and we will agree on a time to meet.

What we do

  • Local GAAP to IFRS transformation. Adjusting entries, reclassifications, explanatory notes.
  • Full IFRS reporting package. Statement of financial position, statement of profit or loss and OCI, cash flow statement, statement of changes in equity, and notes.
  • Audit and due diligence support. Documentation and working files for auditors, answers to their queries, additional disclosures.
  • Reporting for external stakeholders. Financial information for banks, investors, shareholders, parent companies and international partners.

How a project runs

1. Analysis. We study your accounting system, identify differences between local GAAP and IFRS, and develop a transformation plan.

2. Transformation. We perform adjusting entries, prepare reclassifications, and build transformation worksheets.

3. Reporting. We prepare a complete set of IFRS financial statements, including notes and disclosures.

4. Audit. We prepare reporting and audit materials.

Estimated timeline: 2–6 weeks, depending on data volume.
