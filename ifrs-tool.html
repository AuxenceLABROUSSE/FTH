2 Data checks

    3 Account mapping

    Each account gets the IFRS category of the FTH model (the account itself or its group). Change a category and every statement follows. A parent account is used only when its sub-accounts carry no amount. Amounts are net, debit minus credit: credit balances in red.

    4 Preliminary statements

    Preliminary, not IFRS financial statements. Your Ukrainian GAAP figures, reclassified into IFRS line items. IFRS recognition and measurement adjustments (revenue, leases, foreign currency, credit losses, deferred tax…) are not made here: step 5 shows which ones apply to you.

    Statement of financial position UAH

    Profit or loss UAH

    From period turnovers of classes 7–9 (closing account 79 excluded). No tax is calculated: the tax line shows only what the trial balance holds.

    5 IFRS readiness

    The trial balance shows part of the picture. Seven quick questions complete it; the map on the right updates as you answer.

    Questions about your company

    Do you rent offices or equipment for more than 12 months?

    Do you invoice or pay in foreign currency?

    Do you develop software you own (products, platforms)?

    Do employees hold share options (ESOP, phantom shares)?

    Do you have subscriptions, milestones or prepaid multi-period contracts?

    Are there loans from or to owners or group companies?

    Is the company part of a group (foreign parent, subsidiaries)?

    Your IFRS adjustment map

      Indicators, not conclusions: each area is confirmed or set aside by an FTH specialist on your full data.

      6 Send to FTH, securely

      The portal seals your trial balance, your mapping choices and your answers into one file encrypted with the FTH public key. Send that file by e-mail or any file share: without the FTH private key, which never leaves FTH, it is unreadable.
